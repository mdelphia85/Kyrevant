# Kyrevant — The Last Light

## Entry description

Kyrevant is a real-time cooperative sci-fi fantasy game for 2–4 players joining from their own browser with a room code. Your unit survives an asteroid strike and crashes on an alien planet. A handwritten note leads you to Elias, whose people have been captured by the rogue Ashfall militia. Choose a lineage and class, rescue the captives, mine resources, construct a shared base, defend the settlement, and carry the fight through three chapters to the Citadel.

## Instructions for judges

1. Open the public game URL. Free hosting may take about a minute to wake up.
2. Enter a callsign and create a room. Share its invite link/code with a second player on another phone or laptop.
3. The squad leader starts; each player chooses a lineage and class and locks in.
4. Skip or watch the short opening. Use the quest log and gold radar marker to find the note near the wreck.
5. Follow the briefing and shared objectives. The HOW TO PLAY button explains controls during a mission.

No player login, game download, or payment is required. All three chapters lead to a shared victory and can be replayed.
